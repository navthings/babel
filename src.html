Each page is 40 lines of 80 characters from a 29-symbol alphabet (a–z, space, comma, full stop), so there are 293200 ≈ 4.7 × 104679 pages. Nothing is stored. An address is turned into its page, and a page back into its address, by a reversible shuffle (an affine map mod 293200, a digit reversal, and a second affine map). 4 walls, 5 shelves per wall, 32 volumes per shelf, 410 pages per volume, as in the story.
